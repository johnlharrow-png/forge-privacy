Forge privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Forge ("the app") is a daily journal published by 556 Media. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app. The short version: it stays on your phone.

What the app stores

When you use Forge, it stores the following only on your device:

  • Your journal entries: the date, mood, energy level, tags and anything you write
  • Photos you choose to add to an entry. Forge keeps its own private copy, so the entry keeps its photo even if you delete the original from your gallery.
  • Your settings: the name you give the app, your reminder time and whether app lock is on

What we collect

Nothing. Forge has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. None of your entries, photos or settings are sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see them.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details, your name or your email address. To decide whether to unlock the app, Forge asks Google Play, on your phone, whether your Google account has an active plan. Google's own privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Camera and photos

Forge only opens your camera or photo library when you tap "Camera" or "Gallery" while writing an entry. It only gets the photos you pick.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Forge schedules a notification on your phone. It's created on the device itself; no notification service or server is involved.

App lock

App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen-lock check. Forge never sees or stores your biometric data or PIN.

Backups

If you've turned on Android's backup on your phone, Android may include Forge's data in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps. That backup is run by Android and Google under your Google account settings, not by us. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete a single entry from the entry screen, or everything at once from Settings → Erase all entries. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

Forge is meant for adults and isn't directed at children.
